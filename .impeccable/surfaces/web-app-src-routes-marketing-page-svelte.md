## Scope

P1 Landing (`/`). Mode: Persuade. Audience: Grade 12 UPCAT and Grade 6 PSHS NCE takers on cheap phones. Action: sign up for a free mock exam. Proof on hand: real UPCAT questions from the bank; no testimonials, user counts or pass rates exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the product by letting the visitor answer one real UPCAT question before signing up; it refuses the SaaS hero of headline, subcopy, button and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: The pinned reference world: mint-to-chalk-yellow gradient field ("Pisara" palette), white 22px-radius cards with soft offset shadows, ink #18171D pill buttons, chalkboard green #1F8A5B accent with chalk-yellow highlights, Geist, the 8-petal Bloom, answer-sheet bubbles as the one product motif.
STORY: The visitor sees a real question, picks an answer, sees the bubble fill and the explanation, understands the app builds whole mock exams like this and leans toward weak topics, then signs up.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left (desktop) / top (phone): one-line promise and Sign up / Log in. Right / below: a live question card on the gradient field with A–D options as answer-sheet bubbles, hero art behind it. Primary action is the ink pill at the end of the promise.
FORM: Pinned by the owner, no concept roll run. Owner's words: "our main design reference would be @PremiumDashboard.jsx" and, for colour, the owner chose the "Pisara" palette after rejecting purple. seed: none (owner-pinned, roll not run)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
